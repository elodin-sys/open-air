# 07 — SU2 8.5 (compressible Euler, stretch cross-check)

## What it is

Stanford/open-source compressible finite-volume CFD (Euler/RANS/adjoint). In
this pipeline it is a **stretch calibration tool**: a 2-D Euler solve of the
wing section at cruise/dash conditions, compared against the 3-D VLM as a
recorded calibration ratio — explicitly not a design-load source.

## Best practices for 2-D inviscid airfoil runs

- Minimum viable config: `SOLVER= EULER`, `MARKER_EULER= ( airfoil )`,
  `MARKER_FAR= ( farfield )`, `MARKER_MONITORING`, `CONV_NUM_METHOD_FLOW= JST`
  (**`MUSCL_FLOW= NO`** — MUSCL is for upwind schemes), `TIME_DISCRE_FLOW=
  EULER_IMPLICIT`, CFL with adaptation, `HISTORY_OUTPUT= (ITER, RMS_RES,
  AERO_COEFF)` so CL/CD land in the history CSV.
- Prebuilt 8.x binaries accept `MESH_FORMAT= SU2|CGNS` only — **not GMSH**;
  write native `.su2`.
- Farfield at 20+ chords; refine LE/TE; `REF_AREA= 1`, `REF_LENGTH= 1` for
  2-D coefficients.
- **"Exit Success" does not mean converged.** Judge by the density-residual
  drop: 5–6 orders for a quotable Euler solution.
- Compressible solvers are stiff below M≈0.3 (no low-Mach preconditioning in
  this simple setup): we floor the CFD Mach at 0.30 and record that caveat in
  the JSON.

## How open-air uses it

[`src/openair/validation/su2_backend.py`](../../src/openair/validation/su2_backend.py):
Gmsh 2-D NACA mesh → native `.su2` writer → config per point (cruise, dash)
→ `SU2_CFD` subprocess (`tools/su2/bin`, 8.5 "Harrier" linux64-omp) → history
CSV parse. Output `results/<case>/su2.json` carries per-point `ok` (ran and
parsed), **`converged`** (rms density ≤ −4 — separate on purpose), `mach_cfd`
vs `mach_requested`, and 2D-vs-3D calibration ratios.

## Check your work

1. `converged` flag and `rms_density`: at ~−1.6 the CL is calibration data,
   not truth. Only quote coefficients with a 5+ order drop.
2. Thin-airfoil sanity at the CFD condition: CL ≈ 2π(α − α₀L)/√(1−M²) per
   radian, within ~10% for an attached 2-D Euler solve on a decent mesh.
3. The 2-D section CL over 3-D wing CL ratio must be > 1 (3-D washes out
   lift); ~1.3–2 is plausible at these ARs. A ratio < 1 means a setup error.
4. Mesh honesty per chapter 06 (markers closed, counts sane).
5. Never compare SU2 Euler CD to the buildup CD0 — Euler has no friction;
   its CD is induced + numerical dissipation only.

## Known lies

- `ok: true` meaning "the binary exited 0 and a CSV parsed" — convergence is
  the separate `converged` flag (kept separate by design; audit F12-class).
- A beautifully small CD from an unconverged run.
- History CSV column names are quoted and padded — parse case-insensitively.

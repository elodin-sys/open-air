Diana 2 flight evidence
=======================

Source: Jurisson, Eussen, de Visser, and de Breuker (2026), "Flexible
Diana 2 Scaled Glider UAV - Aeroelastic Flight Test Measurements",
4TU.ResearchData version 1.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.4121/0c3fcef0-5b63-480c-ae40-3ff726c657e9.v1
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).

The six training MAT files are locally extracted under truth/training by
scripts/seal_diana2_intake.py. They are intentionally Git-ignored because
their combined size is approximately 1.5 GB. Their immutable SHA-256 values,
the source archive hash, the committed pre-payload split revision, and the
external validation/reserve boundaries are recorded in
inputs/intake-record.yaml.

FT06 and FT12 are never placed in this directory. They are held under the
repository-external diana2-flight holdout directory. FT09 is separately held
as a deferred reserve.
